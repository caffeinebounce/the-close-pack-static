# Skill: QuickBooks Online journal entries

## Prefer the bank register

Ordinary Mercury or CSV activity belongs on the bank account register (or as a matched bank-feed line). Use Journal Entry only when there is no bank line.

## Expense and deposit

- Outflow: Expense or Check against the bank account, payee if known, category from the map, receipt attached when you have one.
- Inflow: Deposit or Sales Receipt / Payment, depending on whether an invoice already exists.

Put the `bank_reference` in the memo.

## Journal Entry

Reserve JE for opening balances, payroll clearing, and corrections. Every JE line needs an account a CPA would recognize. Narrate the reason.

## Owner activity

Owner’s draw / Owner’s investment — not “Office expense.”

## Do not duplicate

Search the register for the bank reference before posting. QBO bank feeds plus a CSV import is the usual source of doubles.
