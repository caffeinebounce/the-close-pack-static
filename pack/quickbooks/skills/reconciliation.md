# Skill: QuickBooks Online reconciliation

## Monthly bank rec

1. Start Reconcile on the operating account.
2. Ending balance and date come from the bank statement, not from memory.
3. Check off cleared lines. Difference must be $0.00 or listed on the exception page.

## Bank feed

- Match suggested lines only when the payee and amount are obviously the same transaction.
- Exclude transfers that already appear on the other account.
- Never “Add” a line you already posted from CSV.

## Open items

Bills, invoices, and unmatched expenses older than 30 days go on the exception table with an owner and a due date.

## Close the period

If your QBO plan supports closing the books, set the closing date to period-end after the package is `final`. Otherwise export the trial balance and stop editing that month.
